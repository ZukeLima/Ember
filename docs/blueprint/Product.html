1 · Executive brief (PRD)

Problem and vision

Problem

An e-commerce operation has customer, order, item, payment and refund data, but its teams cannot safely compare revenue and conversion metrics because records may be duplicated, statuses change after purchase, and KPI definitions differ.

Product vision

Deliver a small, trustworthy analytics platform in which revenue can be traced from source records through tested transformation models to a dashboard with documented definitions and freshness.

Users and jobs-to-be-done

PersonaJobEvidence of success Business analystAnalyze sales by day, product and customer segmentConsistent metrics across dashboard and SQL Finance stakeholderReconcile approved sales, cancellations and refundsEvery aggregate can be explained at order level Analytics engineerSafely rerun and change transformationsDeterministic pipeline, tests, clear lineage and runbook

Goals (MVP)

  1. Reproducible ingestion of synthetic data into local DuckDB.
  2. Tested dbt staging and dimensional marts.
  3. Explicit financial metric contracts and reconciliation.
  4. Dashboard with date/product filters, freshness status and quality warnings.
  5. CI that validates the project from a clean checkout.
  6. Documentation of trade-offs, lineage and recovery steps.

Non-goals (MVP)

  • Real-time streaming, Kubernetes, paid cloud infrastructure or login/roles.
  • Production-grade personal-data handling or a public multi-tenant service.
  • ML recommendations, marketing attribution or mobile app.
  • Payment processing and operational order management.

Success metrics and release gates

MetricMVP target / verification ReproducibilityA documented command sequence creates the whole environment from clean checkout Referential integrityAll accepted order items reference a valid order; invalid records are quarantined or stop publication IdempotencyRunning ingestion + transformation twice with unchanged inputs yields the same business results Financial reconciliationNet revenue equals eligible item revenue minus recognized refunds, within currency rounding precision Quality gateCI fails for critical contract violations and passes with clean fixtures ObservabilityLast successful run, row counts and test status are inspectable

No numeric latency or throughput SLO is committed until the sample data volume and target machine are measured.

Definition of Ready

Every story has a scenario, input/output example, grain and dependencies, explicit acceptance tests and known edge cases. Financial story definitions must be signed off before implementation.

Definition of Done

Implementation merged, local verification run, automated test added, docs updated, CI green and demo evidence captured. A pretty dashboard without validated models is not done.

7 · First implementation ticket

US-01, Deterministic synthetic data generator and fixtures

Task

Build deterministic synthetic data generator and fixtures.

Inputs

Configurable seed, base date and row counts.

Outputs

customers, products, orders, order_items, refunds, with a documented schema and a fixture containing duplicates, partial refunds, late updates and invalid foreign keys.

Acceptance

Same seed/config → byte-identical sorted output; tests assert uniqueness in the canonical clean fixture and explicitly verify invalid fixtures; timestamps use UTC; no sensitive real data.

Out of scope

dbt models, dashboard, cloud hosting.
